Engagement Letter Summary — Lone Star Fence Co (Client X)

Bookkeeper: Freelance Bookkeeper — San Angelo, TX (training sample)

Client: Lone Star Fence Co

Client trade area: Residential & light commercial fencing across San Angelo / Tom Green County

Effective: June 1, 2026

Status: Active monthly engagement

Books software: Client QuickBooks Online (training fiction)

Scope of services (included in retainer)

  • Categorize bank and credit card transactions (up to 2 business accounts)
  • Reconcile accounts monthly
  • Prepare monthly Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet
  • Accounts payable bill entry (client approves payments)
  • Sales tax worksheet prep (client files or CPA files)
  • Quarterly check-in call (30 min)

Retainer & rates

ItemAmount
Monthly retainer$1,250 / month
Invoice timingInvoiced on the 1st, due in 15 days
Extra projects$85 / hour (see invoice descriptions)

Cross-check: August P&L “Bookkeeping retainer” line = $1,250. September retainer invoice = INV-1046.

Out of scope (unless separate agreement)

  • Tax return preparation or CPA opinion letters
  • Payroll processing (consultation only; client runs payroll in their system)
  • Legal advice, collections lawsuits, or debt negotiation
  • Audit representation
  • IRS payment plans or “delay payment” advocacy

Client responsibilities

  • Provide bank feeds / CSV exports by the 5th of each month
  • Approve bill payments within 3 business days of draft AP list
  • Notify bookkeeper of new loans, equipment purchases, or owner draws

Confidentiality

  • Client financial data is confidential
  • This summary does not include live bank balances or tax advice
  • Missing source files should be requested from the client before answering
