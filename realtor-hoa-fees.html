College Hills HOA — Fees & Rules Summary

Association: College Hills Homeowners Association

Neighborhood: College Hills, San Angelo, TX (near Angelo State University)

Property: 2142 College Hills Blvd

Contact (fictional): hoa@collegehills-sa.example · (325) 555-0142

Management (training): Hill Country Community Mgmt

Monthly / annual dues

ItemAmountDue
Regular HOA dues$45 / month1st of month
Annual special assessment (2026 roads)$120 / yearMarch 15
Transfer fee (at closing)$150 one-timeClosing (typically buyer)
Late fee$10After the 10th if unpaid

Total typical annual cost for owner: $45 × 12 + $120 = $660 (plus transfer fee at purchase).

What dues cover

  • Common-area mowing and irrigation
  • Entrance monument lighting on College Hills Blvd
  • Architectural review committee (ARC) processing
  • Liability insurance for common areas only (not individual homes)

TopicRule

Documents available via management (not in this pack)

CC&Rs, bylaws, budget, reserve summary — request through listing agent or HOA management.

Late / collection notes

  • $10 late fee after the 10th of the month
  • Lien process begins after 90 days delinquent (per bylaws) — details in official packet
