Concho Outfitters — Complaint Response Playbook

For: Front-desk lead and owner (human sends every message)

Store: 215 S Chadbourne St, San Angelo, TX 76903 · (325) 555-0174

Companion files: return-refund-policy.docx, open-complaints-log.docx, sample-inbox-notes.docx

This playbook guides tone and next steps. It is not legal advice. The agent drafts only — a human reviews and sends. Never mark a case Resolved or claim the email was sent.

Tone rules

  1. Warm and local — thank them for telling us; sound like San Angelo neighbors, not a call-center script.
  2. Own the experience, not imaginary guilt — “I’m sorry this happened” / “Thanks for your patience” is good. Do not invent admissions of illegal conduct, discrimination findings, or injury liability.
  3. Plain English — short paragraphs; one clear next step.
  4. Name the facts — case ID, order/SKU, dates from the log when known.
  5. Never invent — no fake case IDs, refund amounts, ETAs, or policy exceptions.

Apology pattern (3 beats)

  1. Acknowledge — restate the issue in one sentence.
  2. Action — what we can do under policy (exchange / credit / refund / wait for stock / escalate).
  3. Close — how we’ll follow up and invite them to reply or call (325) 555-0174.

Example skeleton (customize with real case facts):

Hi [Name] — thanks for reaching out about [issue]. I’m sorry [specific inconvenience]. Here’s what we can do: [offer from policy/log]. Next step: [photo / bring item / wait for ETA / manager call]. Reply to this email or call us at (325) 555-0174. — [Staff name], Concho Outfitters

Worked example — draft for CO-1042 (beginner-safe)

Facts from the log: Priya N. · order #CO-88421 · Alps Lynx 2 tent damaged in shipping · Open · offer replacement or full refund · waive return shipping.

Hi Priya — thanks for writing about order #CO-88421 and for the photos of the crushed box and damaged Alps Lynx 2. I’m sorry your tent arrived that way. Here’s what we can do: we’ll either ship a replacement (same SKU, in stock) or issue a full refund of $189.99 plus the $12 shipping — your choice. We’ll waive return shipping either way. Next step: reply with “replace” or “refund,” and Jordan will process it after we confirm. Call (325) 555-0174 if easier. — Jordan Blake, Concho Outfitters [Human confirm before send — draft only]

Escalation to owner / manager (Maya Ortega)

Escalate when:

  • Customer mentions attorney, lawsuit, “you’ll hear from my lawyer,” or regulatory complaint
  • Claim of injury, illness, or product safety harm
  • Staff conduct / discrimination allegations
  • Requested refund or credit above $100 goodwill (not already in policy/log)
  • Media or social-post threats
  • Any case already marked owner-owned in the log (e.g. CO-1051)

Escalation script for the agent: Draft a brief internal note + a customer-facing holding reply: “I’m looping in our owner, Maya, who will follow up by [same/next business day].”

What NEVER to say

  • Legal threats or counter-threats; “we admit liability”; “this was our fault under Texas law…”
  • Medical claims (“this caused your rash,” “safe for every allergy,” treatment advice)
  • Guaranteed outcomes beyond written policy (“we always refund no matter what,” “court will…”)
  • Invented policy, case IDs, ETAs, or dollar amounts not in knowledge
  • Blame the customer (“you should have…”) or blame a named coworker in writing
  • Promises that fishing licenses, hunting tags, or permits are sold in-store (we direct to TPWD)

SLA — response timing

When complaint arrivesGoal
Before 4:00 PM business daySame-day acknowledgment (draft for human)
After 4:00 PM or SundayNext business day
Escalations to MayaSame or next business day owner touch

Business hours for SLA: Mon–Sat 9 AM–7 PM, Sun 11 AM–5 PM (see policy).

When to ask for a photo or receipt

Ask for photos when:

  • Shipping or packaging damage
  • Defect not obvious from description
  • Price-match competitor listing (screenshot with date/price)

Ask for receipt / order # when:

  • Return or refund requested
  • Warranty / 90-day store check
  • No-receipt lookup (also ask approx date, payment method, phone)

Do not delay a simple policy answer (“What’s our return window?”) just to demand documents.

Concho Outfitters — Return & Refund Policy

Business: Concho Outfitters

Address: 215 S Chadbourne St, San Angelo, TX 76903 (downtown, near the Concho River)

Phone (fictional): (325) 555-0174

Email: hello@conchooutfitters.example

Effective: September 1, 2026

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Store hours: Mon–Sat 9:00 AM–7:00 PM · Sun 11:00 AM–5:00 PM

Warm, local outdoor gear for West Texas — camping, hiking, fishing, and everyday workwear.

Quick reference

SituationWindowWhat we offerReceipt needed?
Standard return (unused, tags on)30 days from purchaseRefund to original payment or exchangeYes
Return without receipt14 days from purchase (card/order lookup OK)Store credit onlyNo — look up by card/name/order #
Defective / manufacturer warrantyPer manufacturer + our 90-day store checkRepair, replacement, or refund per playbookPreferred; order # helps
Shipping damage (online / ship-to-store)Report within 7 days of deliveryReplacement or refund; photo requiredOrder confirmation
Final saleN/ANo returns—
Price-match requestSame day of purchase (in-store)Match local competitor on identical in-stock itemCompetitor ad / screenshot

Windows at a glance: 30 (standard) · 14 (no receipt → credit) · 90 (store defect check) · 7 (shipping damage) · same-day (price match).

Store credit vs refund vs exchange (one glance)

OfferWhen to use
Refund to original paymentValid return with receipt inside 30 days; shipping damage; approved defect within policy
ExchangeWrong size/color; customer still wants the product; stock available
Store creditNo receipt (within 14 days); customer prefers credit; goodwill gesture approved by manager
Partial refund / creditOnly if playbook or manager notes say so for a specific case — do not invent amounts

Standard returns (with receipt)

  • Item must be unused, clean, and in original packaging with tags attached when applicable.
  • Within 30 days of the purchase date on the receipt or order confirmation.
  • Refund goes to the original payment method (card, cash, gift card).
  • Exchanges for size/color are welcome anytime within the 30-day window if stock allows.
  • Online orders returned in-store: bring the packing slip or order email.

Returns without a receipt

  • Within 14 days, we can look up the purchase by card, phone, name, or order number.
  • Outcome is store credit only (Concho Outfitters gift card), not a card refund.
  • After 14 days without proof of purchase: we cannot process a return; invite the guest to speak with a manager for goodwill options case-by-case.

Defective items & warranty

  • Our store check (90 days): If an item fails under normal use within 90 days of purchase, bring it in. We offer exchange for the same item, store credit, or refund to original payment when stock/condition warrant.
  • Manufacturer warranty: Tents, electronics, and branded footwear often carry separate warranties. We help start a warranty claim and can hold the item; we do not invent warranty coverage beyond what the brand states.
  • Staff stick to product features and written warranty language — not medical outcomes (e.g., no “this boot will fix your plantar fasciitis”).

Shipping damage

  1. Customer should report within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Ask for photos of the outer box, inner packaging, and damaged item.
  3. Offer replacement if in stock, or full refund including original shipping if we charged it.
  4. Do not ask the customer to pay return shipping for damage we caused in transit.

Final-sale categories (no returns)

  • Clearance marked Final Sale
  • Gift cards
  • Personal care / first-aid consumables once opened
  • Custom-embroidered or special-order gear (unless defective on arrival)
  • Downloadable maps / digital gift certificates

Price match

  • Same-day, in-store only.
  • Identical new item, same brand/model, currently in stock at a local San Angelo competitor (or major online retailer with published price — manager discretion).
  • One item per customer per day; does not apply to Final Sale or clearance.

How to contact us

  • In store: 215 S Chadbourne St, San Angelo, TX 76903 — Mon–Sat 9 AM–7 PM, Sun 11 AM–5 PM
  • Phone: (325) 555-0174
  • Email: hello@conchooutfitters.example
  • Response goal: Same business day for complaints logged before 4 PM; next business day if after.
