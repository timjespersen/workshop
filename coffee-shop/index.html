Allergen & Dietary Notes — Concho River Roasters

Last updated: September 2026

Important: Kitchen is small and shared. Cross-contact is possible. Guests with severe allergies should speak with staff before ordering. This file is not medical advice.

Common allergens present in shop

AllergenWhere it appears
Milk / dairyLatte, cappuccino, mocha, chai, hot chocolate, banana bread, muffins, breakfast tacos with cheese
EggsBreakfast tacos, banana bread, muffins, avocado toast mayo option
Wheat / glutenBanana bread, muffins, sourdough toast, grilled PB&J, flour taco tortillas
Tree nutsAlmond milk; Pecan Pie Latte syrup may contain tree-nut flavoring (check bottle with staff)
SoySome syrups; ask staff for current syrup brands
PeanutsPB&J sandwich; peanut butter jar near prep — not a peanut-free facility

Better options for common requests

RequestGuidance from this file
Dairy-free drinksOat or almond milk; drip coffee and americano are dairy-free as served black
Gluten-consciousCorn tortillas available for tacos on request; no certified gluten-free bread
VeganBlack coffee/americano; oat milk drinks; avocado toast without butter (request olive oil)
Peanut-awareAvoid PB&J; note shared prep area — staff can wipe steamer wand on request (still not a guarantee)

Cross-contact & shop limits

  • Concho River Roasters is not an allergen-free, peanut-free, or “guaranteed safe” kitchen.
  • Severe allergy questions are handled in person with staff; guests may call (325) 555-0198 before visiting.
  • Staff share ingredient labels on request; they do not diagnose allergies or recommend medical treatment.
